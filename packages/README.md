# Packages

Packages are curated entry points for a specific audience or outcome. They
combine the repository's reusable skills with explanations, exercises,
templates, and adoption guidance.

## Available packages

| Package | Audience | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [AI Best Practices](ai-best-practices/README.md) | Operators, PMs, engineers, and team leads | Use AI reliably across planning, coding, debugging, QA, communication, and multi-project work |

New packages should be audience-specific, self-contained, and useful without
requiring access to SGS's private systems.
