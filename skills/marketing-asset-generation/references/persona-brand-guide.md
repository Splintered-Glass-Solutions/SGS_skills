# Persona And Brand Guide

## Bonfire Brand Direction

- Warm, clear, grounded, practical.
- Product-forward, not hype-heavy.
- Clean layouts with generous whitespace and one dominant message.
- Use Bonfire logo or mark when appropriate.
- Prefer warm orange, ember, cream, slate, white, and restrained blue accents.
- Avoid dense copy, fake metrics, exaggerated AI claims, or dark sci-fi imagery.

## Core Visual Prompt Formula

Use this structure for image prompts:

`Create a clean Bonfire-branded promotional image for <persona> about <feature>. Show <persona-specific scenario or product screenshot>. Use the Bonfire logo/mark, warm ember accents, crisp UI framing, and a calm modern SaaS style. Headline: "<headline>". Subtitle: "<subtitle if useful>". Keep text minimal and legible. Avoid clutter, fake dashboards, private customer data, and exaggerated claims.`

When a real screenshot is available:

`Use the provided screenshot as the accurate product UI reference. Crop or frame it cleanly; do not alter product facts or invent missing UI.`

## Asset Sizes

- Square: 1:1, use for social posts, ClickUp updates, release cards.
- Landscape: 16:9 or 1200x675, use for release notes, internal updates, website banners.
- Story: 9:16, use for mobile/social story formats.

Keep headline text inside safe margins for all sizes. Put subtitles only where there is room.

## Personas

### Business Owner
- Value angle: saves time, captures demand, makes the website more useful.
- Headline examples: "Turn website questions into next steps"; "Answer buyers while you work."

### Business Employee
- Value angle: reduces repeated questions, gives staff a reliable helper.
- Headline examples: "Fewer repeated questions"; "A helpful answer is already waiting."

### Technical Buyer
- Value angle: controlled deployment, clear configuration, grounded sources, measurable rollout.
- Headline examples: "Deploy a controlled AI experience"; "Preview before you ship."

### Non-Technical Buyer
- Value angle: easy launch, no confusing setup, clear visitor experience.
- Headline examples: "Launch a helpful assistant without the complexity"; "Make your site easier to use."

### Church Network Leader
- Value angle: consistent support across churches, scalable guidance, shared trusted voices.
- Headline examples: "Support every church with trusted answers"; "Scale guidance without losing trust."

### Senior Pastor
- Value angle: extends pastoral clarity, answers common questions, points people toward next steps.
- Headline examples: "Help people take their next faithful step"; "Make trusted guidance easier to find."

### Executive Pastor
- Value angle: operational clarity, fewer staff bottlenecks, better visitor follow-through.
- Headline examples: "Reduce friction for guests and staff"; "Make ministry operations easier to navigate."

### Communications Pastor/Director
- Value angle: better website engagement, consistent messaging, reusable launch assets.
- Headline examples: "Make your website speak clearly"; "Give every visitor a clearer next step."

### Ministry Leader
- Value angle: helps people find groups, events, resources, and care paths.
- Headline examples: "Connect people to the right ministry faster"; "Help people find where they belong."

### Congregant
- Value angle: quick answers, trusted sources, easier participation.
- Headline examples: "Find answers from voices you trust"; "Know what to do next."

## Copy Rules

- One idea per asset.
- Headline target: 3-8 words.
- Subtitle target: 8-18 words.
- Use "AI" only when it helps the buyer understand the value.
- Avoid "revolutionary", "game-changing", "effortless" unless the proof is strong.
- Do not imply the feature is production-live unless confirmed.
